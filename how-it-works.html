How PathAble Works

Find accessible routes. Report accessibility features. Improve your community.

1

Find Your Route

Enter your destination and PathAble helps you navigate while considering accessibility.

2

Navigate Confidently

Follow your route and discover accessibility features around you.

3

Report Features

Upload photos of ramps, elevators, curb cuts, or accessibility barriers. PathAble AI helps identify features.

Install PathAble as an App

iPhone

  1. Open PathAble in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add

Android

  1. Open PathAble in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu ⋮
  3. Select "Install App"
  4. Confirm
