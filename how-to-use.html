How PathAble works

Find accessible routes. Report accessibility features. Improve your community.

1

Find your route

Enter your destination and PathAble helps you navigate while considering accessibility along the way.

2

Navigate confidently

Follow your route and see accessibility features, like ramps and curb cuts, mapped around you.

3

Report features

Upload a photo of a ramp, elevator, curb cut, or accessibility barrier. PathAble automatically identifies what's in the photo.

Install PathAble as an app

iPhone

  1. Open PathAble in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add

Android

  1. Open PathAble in Chrome
  2. Tap the menu icon
  3. Select "Install App"
  4. Confirm
